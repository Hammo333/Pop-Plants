## Quick Care Overview
* **Light:** Bright, indirect sunlight. Avoid direct, scorching sun which burns foliage.
* **Water:** Every 1–2 weeks. Water when the top 2 inches of soil feel dry to the touch.
* **Humidity & Temperature:** Thrives in average to high humidity (50%+); prefers temperatures between 18°C–30°C (65°F–85°F).
* **Pro Tip:** Wipe the large leaves with a damp cloth monthly to clear dust and maximize photosynthesis.

## Detailed Care & Maintenance
* **Soil & Potting:** Rich, chunky potting mix containing peat moss, perlite, and orchid bark for high aeration. Use a pot with generous drainage holes and add a moss pole or trellis to support climbing stems.
* **Fertilizer:** Feed monthly during spring and summer with a balanced, liquid houseplant fertilizer diluted to half strength.

## Safety & Toxicity
* **Pet & Child Safety:** **Toxic.** Contains insoluble calcium oxalate crystals that cause mouth irritation, drooling, and swallowing difficulty if chewed by cats, dogs, or children.

## Common Troubleshooting / FAQ
* **Why are there no split leaves (fenestrations)?** Young foliage starts solid; fenestrations develop as the plant matures under bright, indirect lighting conditions.
* **Why are leaf tips turning brown and crispy?** Crispy edges usually indicate low ambient humidity or underwatering.
* **Is it susceptible to specific pests?** Watch for thrips, spider mites, and scale on the undersides of large leaf blades.
