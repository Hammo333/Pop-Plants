## Quick Care Overview
* **Light:** Tolerates low light to bright, indirect sunlight. Avoid extreme direct sun.
* **Water:** Every 2–4 weeks. Allow soil to dry completely between waterings; overwatering causes root rot.
* **Humidity & Temperature:** Prefers normal to dry room humidity; keeps best between 15°C–29°C (60°F–85°F).
* **Pro Tip:** When in doubt, don't water! Snake plants are extremely drought-tolerant.

## Detailed Care & Maintenance
* **Soil & Potting:** Well-draining cactus or succulent soil mix. Use a heavy terra cotta pot to prevent tall leaves from tipping over.
* **Fertilizer:** Feed once or twice during spring and summer with a light succulent fertilizer.

## Safety & Toxicity
* **Pet & Child Safety:** **Toxic.** Contains saponins which can cause nausea, vomiting, and diarrhea in dogs and cats.

## Common Troubleshooting / FAQ
* **Why are the leaves yellowing or mushy?** This is a classic sign of overwatering or soil retaining too much moisture.
* **Why are the leaves leaning over?** Usually caused by inadequate light or root issues from soggy soil.
